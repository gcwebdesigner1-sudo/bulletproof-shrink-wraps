Shrink wrap for whatever you need to keep dry, clean or in one piece.

Bulletproof Shrink Wraps has been wrapping boats, RVs, equipment and buildings around North Idaho since 2011. We come to you with industrial polyethylene film and rubber adhesive tape, and wrap it where it sits, on land or on the water.

  1. Winter storage wraps

    Boats, RVs, cars, ATVs and tractors wrapped for the off season. Snow slides off, mice and birds stay out, and the sun doesn't cook your upholstery. Done in your driveway, shop or storage barn.

  2. Wraps on the water

    Boat still on the lift? Boathouse on the dock? We wrap from the water too, including floatplanes.

  3. Transport wraps

    Built to take highway wind for hundreds of miles. Customers have towed boats across the country with our wraps and pulled in with nothing torn or shifted. We also wrap oversize loads for the trucking companies hauling them.

  4. Homes, sheds and shelters

    Shrink wrap closes up a building under construction, a roof waiting on repairs, or a hoop shelter for the animals. It goes up fast and stands up to wind and snow.

  5. Commercial and industrial

    Aviation, energy, mining, military and law enforcement. Machinery, parts and equipment wrapped for storage or shipping, in white, blue or clear film.

How a wrap goes on.

Four steps, start to finish. Keep scrolling and watch one go on.

  1. 01

    Band and frame

    A strap band goes around the hull under the rub rail. Then we build a support frame so the top pitches and snow slides off instead of piling up.

  2. 02

    Drape the film

    One big sheet of industrial polyethylene goes over the top and gets welded to the band all the way around.

  3. 03

    Heat it tight

    The heat gun shrinks the film until it's drum tight against the frame. No flapping, no pockets for water to sit in.

  4. 04

    Seal it up

    Seams get polyethylene rubber adhesive tape. Vents and a zip door go in where they make sense, so you can still get inside.

Questions people ask before they book.

How much does it cost to shrink wrap a boat?

It's priced by the foot. Storage on land starts at $24 a foot, storage on the water at $26, transport at $32 and commercial at $35. A 20 foot boat wrapped for storage on land starts around $480. More than 100 miles round trip adds a flat $100 fuel fee.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We wrap in driveways, barns, storage lots and marinas, and on lifts and docks, all over North Idaho and the Spokane area.

Can you wrap my boat while it's still in the water?

Yes. That's the on-water storage wrap, for boats on a lift or tied up at the dock. Boathouses and floatplanes too.

Will the wrap hold up on the highway?

A transport wrap is strapped, heat shrunk tight and taped so it doesn't flap or catch wind. Customers have towed boats across the country with them and arrived with the wrap still in one piece.

What else can you wrap?

RVs, cars and trucks, ATVs, tractors and big equipment, homes, sheds and hoop shelters, plus industrial work for aviation, energy, mining, military and law enforcement.

Who does the work?

Chris owns the company and does the wrapping himself. Bulletproof has been at it since 2011.
